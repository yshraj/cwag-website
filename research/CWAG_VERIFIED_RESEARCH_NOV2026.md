## 1. Gujarat Districts Count

### Verified Claim: **34 Districts**

**Source:** [Wikipedia - List of districts of Gujarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_districts_of_Gujarat)

Gujarat has **34 districts** as of October 2025, after Vav-Tharad was formed from parts of Banaskantha on October 2, 2025.

**Historical context:**
- 1960: Gujarat formed with 17 districts
- 1997: 5 new districts created
- 2007: Tapi became 26th district
- 2013: 7 new districts (total 33)
- 2025: Vav-Tharad created (total 34)

**Website Status:** ✅ Updated to 34 districts
