## Important Notes

1. **Editorial Images:** The research document references Gujarat Jagran, Divya Bhaskar, and Cricketnmore photographs. These are NOT included in this asset collection as they require explicit permission from publishers.

2. **CWAG-Owned Assets:** No verified CWAG-owned images, logos, or photographs have been obtained. The website currently uses stock photography as placeholders.

3. **Minors in Photography:** Some coaching/sports stock images may feature young athletes. These are licensed stock photos with appropriate model releases. CWAG-specific photographs featuring minors would require separate consent.

4. **Attribution:** While Unsplash does not require attribution, it is appreciated. Consider adding an image credits page or footer acknowledgment.
