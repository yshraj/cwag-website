# CWAG Website Reference Research

## Research Summary

This document contains the analysis of reference websites and CWAG's digital presence to inform the design and implementation of the Coaches Welfare Association Gujarat official website.

---

## 1. Reference Websites Analysed

### A. Sports Authority of India (SAI)

#### 1.1 SAI Main Portal (sportsauthorityofindia.gov.in)
**Status:** ✅ Successfully accessed

**Key Observations:**
- **Header:** Features government ministerial hierarchy prominently (PM, Sports Minister, MoS)
- **Visual Style:** Professional government portal aesthetic with blue primary colors
- **Statistics Display:** Animated counters showing Athletes (0+), Coaches (0+), Training Centers (0+), Regional Centers (0), Promotional Schemes (0), NCOEs (0+)
- **Hero Section:** Features leadership carousel with photos of government officials
- **Timeline Section:** "SAI Journey" from 1982 Asian Games to 2025 Khelo Bharat Niti - excellent storytelling approach
- **About Section:** Clear vision and mission statements
- **Key Initiatives:** Cards displaying "Fitness & Inclusivity", "Indigenous & Tribal Games", "Para Sports"
- **Regional Centers Map:** Interactive display showing 10 regional centres and 2 academic institutions
- **Partners Section:** Logo carousel of affiliated organizations
- **Footer:** Government standard footer with links

**Design Patterns Worth Adopting:**
- Leadership showcase with photos and titles
- Animated statistics counters
- Timeline for organizational history
- Key initiatives cards
- Regional presence visualization
- Partners/affiliations section

#### 1.2 SAI Directory (sportsauthorityofindia.nic.in/directory)
**Status:** ✅ Successfully accessed (large page)

**Key Observations:**
- Comprehensive "Who's Who" directory
- Hierarchical structure showing organizational positions
- Photo-based directory entries
- Clear designation and contact information format
- Searchable/filterable interface

**Design Patterns Worth Adopting:**
- Photo-based directory cards
- Hierarchical organization display
- Clear contact information formatting

#### 1.3 SAI Academic Institutions
**Status:** ✅ Successfully accessed

**Key Observations:**
- Two main institutions: NSNIS Patiala and LNCPE Thiruvananthapuram
- Clean card-based layout for institution listing
- "More Information" CTAs for detailed views
- Partner organization logos at bottom

**Design Patterns Worth Adopting:**
- Institution cards with images
- Clear CTA buttons
- Organized link structure

#### 1.4 SAI Courses (sportsauthorityofindia.gov.in/courses)
**Status:** ✅ Successfully accessed

**Key Observations:**
- Clear course categorization:
  - Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd - 4 years)
  - Master of Physical Education (MPE - 2 years)
  - Master of Philosophy (Physical Education)
  - Post Graduate Diploma in Health & Fitness Management
  - Diploma in Sports Coaching
  - MSc in Sports Coaching
  - Refresher courses
  - Post Graduate Diploma in Sports Medicine
- Each course shows duration, affiliation, career prospects
- Clear hierarchy of educational offerings

**Design Patterns Worth Adopting:**
- Course cards with clear information hierarchy
- Eligibility and career scope information
- Organized course categories

## 4. Recommended Visual Direction

### 4.1 Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Deep Blue (#1e3a8a) - Trust, professionalism, sports authority
- Saffron/Orange (#f97316) - Energy, Gujarat identity, Indian sports
- White (#ffffff) - Clean backgrounds

**Secondary Colors:**
- Light Blue (#3b82f6) - Accents
- Green (#22c55e) - Success, growth
- Dark Gray (#1f2937) - Text

**Gujarati Theme Consideration:**
- Incorporate subtle saffron/orange elements
- Balance government authority (blue) with cultural identity

### 4.2 Typography
- **Display Font:** DM Sans (from template) - Modern, clean
- **Body Font:** Inter - Excellent readability
- **Gujarati Font:** Noto Sans Gujarati - Best web support

### 4.3 Overall Aesthetic
- Professional sports federation look
- Clean, organized layouts
- High contrast for accessibility
- Subtle animations (not flashy)
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Government credibility feel without being outdated

## 5. Proposed Website Architecture

### 5.1 Site Map

```
Homepage (/)
├── About (/about)
│   ├── About CWAG
│   ├── Vision & Mission
│   ├── Objectives
│   ├── Leadership
│   └── History
├── Coach Directory (/directory)
│   ├── Search & Filter
│   └── Coach Profiles
├── Membership (/membership)
│   ├── Benefits
│   ├── Categories
│   ├── Eligibility
│   └── How to Join
├── Registration (/register)
│   └── Coach Registration Form
├── News & Announcements (/news)
│   └── Individual articles
├── Events (/events)
│   ├── Upcoming Events
│   └── Past Events
├── Contact (/contact)
│   ├── Contact Form
│   └── District Contacts
└── Resources (/resources)
    ├── Government Schemes
    └── Useful Links
```

### 5.2 Homepage Sections (in order)

1. **Hero Section**
   - CWAG logo and name
   - Tagline (English/Gujarati)
   - Key statistics
   - Primary CTAs (Register, Learn More)

2. **About CWAG** (brief)
   - Mission statement
   - Key objectives
   - CTA to full About page

3. **Why Join CWAG**
   - Membership benefits cards
   - CTA to Membership page

4. **Coach Directory Preview**
   - Featured coaches
   - Search CTA
   - Sports categories

5. **Gujarat Districts**
   - Visual representation of statewide presence
   - District statistics

6. **News & Announcements**
   - Latest 3-4 news items
   - View all CTA

7. **Upcoming Events**
   - Next 2-3 events
   - Events calendar CTA

8. **Affiliations & Partners**
   - Sports Authority logos
   - Government affiliations

9. **Join CTA Section**
   - Final membership push
   - Registration form shortcut

10. **Footer**
    - Full navigation
